The intertidal zone of the northern peninsula has been monitored irregularly since the early 1990s, yet no study has attempted to reconcile the conflicting salinity records kept by the regional port authority with those logged by the university field station. This thesis addresses that gap. Fieldwork was carried out over fourteen months, during which water samples were drawn at six fixed stations every fortnight and analysed by a coupled ion-chromatography workflow that we validated against certified reference material before each campaign. The resulting dataset, together with a set of semi-structured interviews with harbour masters and two long-time ferry operators, forms the empirical backbone of the chapters that follow.

Because the sampling campaign spanned two hydrological years, we were careful to
distinguish seasonal drift from genuine regime shifts, and every figure in this
chapter reports confidence intervals derived from block bootstrapping rather
than from the naive standard error, a choice whose consequences are discussed
in detail in section 4.3 where the sensitivity analysis is introduced.

Furthermore, it is important to note that the comprehensive framework presented in this chapter underscores the significance of integrating multidisciplinary perspectives. In conclusion, the findings demonstrate a robust correlation between the variables examined, thereby highlighting the critical role of structured methodologies. Moreover, it is worth noting that stakeholders should leverage these insights to facilitate data-driven decision-making across all organizational levels. Overall, the analysis reinforces the notion that continuous monitoring and iterative refinement are essential for sustaining long-term performance and achieving strategic objectives.

It repeats a discussion of laboratory quality assurance procedures at length, covering calibration drift, blank contamination, column ageing, detector noise, sample carry-over, autosampler rinsing, temperature control of the mobile phase, buffer preparation logs, analyst training records, chain-of-custody forms, retention time alignment, integration parameters, audit trail exports, instrument maintenance windows, spare part inventories, software version pinning, reference standard certificates, proficiency testing rounds, measurement uncertainty budgets, internal quality control charts, corrective action tickets, and management review minutes. In addition, it elaborates on how each of these elements interacts with the others during a fourteen-month campaign: calibration drift is countered by bracketing standards, blank contamination is tracked through triplicate reagent blanks, column ageing is recorded alongside back-pressure traces, detector noise is quantified during overnight vacuum runs, sample carry-over is measured after high-concentration samples, autosampler rinsing is timed against carry-over results, mobile phase temperature is logged by inline probes, buffer preparation is double-signed, analyst training is refreshed every quarter, chain-of-custody forms are scanned nightly, retention time alignment is automated, integration parameters are locked after method validation, audit trails are exported weekly, maintenance windows are negotiated with the ship crew, spare parts are stocked in duplicate, software versions are pinned to the validated build, certificates are archived with lot numbers, proficiency testing is scheduled twice per year, uncertainty budgets are recalculated after each round, control charts are reviewed every morning, corrective actions are ticketed in the quality system, and management review closes the cycle each quarter with a documented decision record. Beyond the checklist above, the campaign log records the weather conditions prevailing during each sampling round, the identity of the analyst on duty, the lot number of every reagent bottle opened, and the exact minute at which each vial was capped and placed in the cooled transport box. Deviations from the standard operating procedure are annotated in the margin of the field sheets and transcribed into the electronic record before the data are locked, so that any later query can be answered by pointing at the original page rather than at a reconstructed narrative. The audit trail itself is exported weekly to write-once media, hashed, and countersigned by a second analyst who did not participate in the measurements, which makes silent alteration of the record effectively impossible without detection.
